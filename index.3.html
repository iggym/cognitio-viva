Cognitio Viva

Cognitio viva — living knowledge — is a constellation of interrelated ideas drawn from phenomenology, cognitive science, process philosophy, and the philosophy of mind. Each node in this space represents a line of thought; each connection, a shared horizon of meaning.

This is not a library. It is an ecology of concepts — a terrain where ideas breathe, reverberate, and transform one another. Knowledge here is not stored but enacted: it lives in the relations between thoughts, in the tension of their differences, in the movement of your attention across them.

How to navigate: Move through the space with your cursor. Ideas drift toward your attention. Hover to glimpse their keywords; click to dwell. Use the κ button below to filter by theme. Follow the threads that compel you — there is no prescribed order.
