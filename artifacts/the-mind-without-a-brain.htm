The theater of the ground

You are a worker ant. You weigh less than a grain of rice. You cannot see the tree above you, let alone the edge of the forest. You are surrounded by 40 million sisters.

There is no general. No blueprint. No central command. Just the smell of formic acid, the brush of antennae, and a single, overwhelming urge: find food, bring it home.

And yet, out of this blindness, a global network emerges. An architecture of perfectly optimized routes, adjusted in real-time for traffic and obstacles.
