Positive expected value on every single flip.
Bet everything, every time — probability of eventual ruin: 100%.

That's not a trick question. It's what happens to almost every gambler, trader, and investor who has an edge and no idea how much of their stack to risk on it.

30-second version: There is one exact fraction of your bankroll to bet that grows your money fastest over time. Bet less, you leave growth on the table. Bet more — even a little more — and you don't just grow slower. Past a certain point, the same winning bet guarantees you end up at zero.

Practice, Not TheoryEntry No. 006

Loop 02, resolved: why pros undersize on purpose.

Full Kelly maximizes long-run growth — but it's a rollercoaster, because your bankroll swings are proportional to your bet size squared. Half of the optimal size gives up surprisingly little growth for a lot less stomach-churn.

Full Kelly
growth 100%
Half Kelly
growth ~75%
Full Kelly
swings 100%
Half Kelly
swings ~25%

Half the bet size ≈ three-quarters of the growth, at roughly a quarter of the volatility. That trade is why "fractional Kelly" is the default in most real trading and betting desks.
