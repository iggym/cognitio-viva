The shape of a summer

Remember summer vacation at age eight. Eight weeks stretched in every direction. July felt like a small eternity. By August you'd forgotten the smell of the school cafeteria, the weight of the backpack, the precise angle of morning light through classroom windows.

Now think about last July. Doesn't it feel like it ended about a week ago?

The clock didn't change. The calendar didn't change. Something inside you did.
