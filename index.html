❖ Cognitio Viva

Derived from classical epistemology (Alexander Baumgarten & G.F. Meier), Cognitio Viva translates to Living Cognition—knowledge that possesses motion, affect, and sufficient power to excite thought and prompt active creation.

Unlike static archives (cognitio mortua or dead knowledge), this platform treats notes, systems blueprints, and contextual thoughts as interconnected, living nodes that evolve dynamically.
